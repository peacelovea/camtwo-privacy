CamTwo 隐私政策

生效日期 2026 年 11 月 8 日

CamTwo 是一款前后双摄拍摄应用。开发者及个人信息处理者为 yubo shi，联系邮箱为 shiyubo.code@foxmail.com。本政策说明使用应用、购买 Pro 或访问本页面时的信息处理方式。

1. 拍摄内容与本机数据

前后摄像头画面、照片、视频和录音在设备上采集、合成和保存。CamTwo 不将这些拍摄内容上传给开发者或 RevenueCat。你可以选择将成片及支持的前后原片保存到系统照片图库。

拍摄设置和购买服务的本机缓存保存在设备中。保存失败时，已完成的素材可能暂存于应用本机目录，供你重试。保存成功后，应用会删除对应的暂存文件。卸载应用可能清除应用目录内尚未保存到图库的素材。

导出的照片可能保留系统提供的拍摄元数据。本应用不请求定位权限。如果你启用了 iCloud 照片或设备备份，Apple 可能依据你的系统设置同步图库或备份本机数据。

2. 系统权限

  • 相机用于前后双摄预览、拍照和录像。
  • 麦克风用于为录像录制声音。拒绝授权时，应用可在设备与当前配置支持的情况下录制无声视频。
  • 添加照片用于将拍摄结果保存到系统图库。当前应用使用仅添加权限，保存功能不需要浏览你的整个照片图库。

你可以在 iPhone 的系统设置中管理这些权限。撤回权限会影响对应的拍摄或保存功能。

3. 购买与 RevenueCat

CamTwo 使用 Apple App 内购买和 RevenueCat 管理 Pro 购买、订阅、恢复购买与权益状态。购买服务会联网处理商品及交易标识、购买记录、订阅状态、价格、币种、商店地区，以及 SDK 生成的应用用户标识。

RevenueCat SDK 还会发送设备型号、系统和应用版本、语言地区及设备供应商标识符等技术信息，网络请求也会向服务提供者暴露 IP 地址。这些信息用于购买验证、权益交付、恢复、服务安全及购买与订阅统计分析。购买服务可能在打开应用和刷新状态时联网。

付款由 Apple 处理。CamTwo 不接收你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账户密码，也未向 RevenueCat 设置姓名、邮箱等自定义客户属性。当前应用没有自建登录账户，没有集成广告 SDK 或开启广告标识符收集。

第三方政策可参阅 Apple 隐私政策和 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

4. 本页面与联系邮件

本页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面时，GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。页面本身没有添加统计脚本、广告、表单或 Cookie。详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。

如果你通过邮件联系开发者，我们会处理你的邮箱、邮件内容及你主动提供的附件，用于回复问题或协助处理隐私请求。请仅发送解决问题所需的信息。

5. 处理目的、保留与跨境处理

我们根据适用法律，以提供你请求的拍摄与购买功能、履行相关交易、维护服务安全、处理支持请求和履行法定义务为目的处理必要信息，并在法律要求时取得同意。

本机媒体由你在应用和系统图库中管理。购买记录与支持邮件会在提供服务、恢复权益、处理争议及满足适用法律要求所必要的期间内保留。Apple、RevenueCat 和 GitHub 按各自适用政策处理其服务数据。

上述服务提供者可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理数据，包括美国。你可以通过本页列出的第三方政策了解相关处理方式，也可以联系开发者咨询适用于 CamTwo 的数据处理安排。

6. 你的选择与权利

你可以撤回系统权限、删除图库中的成片或卸载应用。卸载应用不会自动取消订阅，也不等于删除 Apple 或 RevenueCat 中依法保留的交易记录。订阅可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理。

根据适用法律，你可以请求访问、更正或删除有关个人信息，或提出限制处理、反对处理、撤回同意和数据可携带等请求，也可以向有管辖权的数据保护机构投诉。请通过邮箱联系开发者，我们会在适用法律要求的范围与期限内处理请求，并在必要时核验你的身份或交易关联。

7. 儿童隐私与政策更新

CamTwo 不以儿童个人信息收集为目的。如果你认为儿童在使用购买服务或联系支持时提供了需要处理的个人信息，请联系开发者。

本政策更新时会在本页面标明新的生效日期。对于涉及数据处理方式的重大变化，我们会按适用法律要求提供通知。

CamTwo Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

CamTwo is a front-and-back camera app. The developer and data controller is yubo shi, reachable at shiyubo.code@foxmail.com. This policy explains information handling when you use the app, purchase Pro, or visit this page.

1. Captures and on-device data

Camera frames, photos, videos, and audio are captured, combined, and saved on your device. CamTwo does not upload this capture content to the developer or RevenueCat. You can save the combined result and supported original camera files to the system photo library.

Capture preferences and local purchase-service caches are stored on the device. Completed media may remain in the app directory after a failed save so that you can retry. After a successful save, the corresponding temporary files are removed. Uninstalling the app may remove media in its directory that has not been saved to your photo library.

Exported photos may retain capture metadata supplied by the system. The app does not request location permission. If you enable iCloud Photos or device backups, Apple may synchronize your library or back up local app data according to your system settings.

2. System permissions

  • Camera access enables front-and-back previews, photos, and video recording.
  • Microphone access records sound for videos. If permission is denied, silent recording may be available depending on the device and configuration.
  • Add Photos access saves your captures to the system library. The app currently uses add-only permission, so saving does not require browsing your entire library.

You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings. Revoking a permission affects the corresponding capture or save feature.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

CamTwo uses Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat for Pro purchases, subscriptions, restoration, and entitlement status. Purchase services process product and transaction identifiers, purchase history, subscription status, prices, currencies, storefront information, and an SDK-generated app user identifier over the network.

The RevenueCat SDK also sends technical information such as device model, operating system and app versions, locale, and the vendor device identifier. Network requests expose your IP address to the service provider. This information supports purchase validation, entitlement delivery, restoration, security, and purchase and subscription analytics. Purchase services may connect when you open the app or refresh its status.

Apple processes payments. CamTwo does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password and does not set custom RevenueCat customer attributes such as your name or email address. The app currently has no developer-operated login account, advertising SDK, or enabled advertising-identifier collection.

See the Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. This page and support emails

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This page itself adds no analytics scripts, advertisements, forms, or cookies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email the developer, we process your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide to respond or assist with privacy requests. Please send only information needed to address your request.

5. Purposes, retention, and international processing

Subject to applicable law, we process necessary information to provide requested capture and purchase features, fulfill related transactions, protect service security, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We obtain consent where required by law.

You manage local media in the app and system photo library. Purchase records and support correspondence are retained as needed to provide services, restore access, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements. Apple, RevenueCat, and GitHub handle their service data under their applicable policies.

These providers may process data outside your country or region, including in the United States. Their policies describe their processing practices. You may also contact the developer about the arrangements applicable to CamTwo.

6. Your choices and rights

You can revoke permissions, remove saved captures from your library, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not automatically cancel a subscription or delete transaction records lawfully retained by Apple or RevenueCat. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or data portability, and may complain to a competent data protection authority. Email the developer to make a request. We handle requests within applicable legal requirements and time limits and may need to verify your identity or connection to a transaction.

7. Children and policy updates

CamTwo is not designed to collect children's personal information. Contact the developer if you believe a child has provided personal information through purchase services or support that requires attention.

Updates will show a new effective date on this page. Material changes to data processing will be notified as required by applicable law.
